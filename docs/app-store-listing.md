# Pawstop App Store listing

## Name
Pawstop

## Subtitle
Road trip breaks for your dog

## Promotional text
Plan useful breaks around the route you're driving, your dog's life stage, and your preferred stop timing.

## Description
Make the drive better for you and your dog.

Pawstop plans breaks along your driving route, helping you find places to pause without spending your trip searching for the next stop.

Enter your starting point and destination, choose your dog's life stage and break cadence, and set how far you're willing to detour. Pawstop looks for real places near each planned break and explains the available evidence behind its recommendations.

• Search for trip locations with suggested places as you type.
• Start from your current location, check the starting pin, or enter an address.
• Adjust break timing and detour preferences.
• Explore stop options with available amenities and clear uncertainty labels.
• Open stops in Google Maps for directions.

Place details and access can change. Pawstop labels unconfirmed information and may leave a break open when it cannot find a suitable match. Driving estimates exclude live traffic. Plan while parked and adjust breaks to your dog's needs.
