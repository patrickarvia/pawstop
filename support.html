Pawstop support

For help or feedback, email patrick@cairngtm.com.

Include your app version, iPhone model, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Share only the location details needed to explain the issue.

Location looks wrong

Use the starting-point map link to check the pin. Choose Change starting location to enter an address, or request your current location again. Check Pawstop's location permission and Precise Location in iOS Settings if needed.

A route or stop is unavailable

Check your internet connection and try a more specific address. Stop access, amenities, opening hours, and road conditions can change. Check the place details before visiting and follow local signs.

Using Pawstop on the road

Plan while parked or ask a passenger to use the app. Break suggestions are planning aids. Adjust stops to your dog's needs and seek veterinary advice for health concerns.
