Privacy policy

Pawstop is provided by Cairn GTM LLC. This policy describes how the app and website handle information when you plan trips.

Trip and location information

When you request suggestions or plan a trip, the text you enter, selected places, trip endpoints, your dog’s name, and relevant preferences are sent to our service. If you choose current location, your device coordinates are used for your starting point, nearby suggestions, and an approximate address lookup. You can enter an address instead and change location permission in your device settings.

Google Maps Platform processes location searches, coordinates, routes, and place information to provide these features. Opening a stop or starting point in Google Maps shares the selected route or coordinates with Google.

Information stored on your device

The iOS app saves your dog's name, life stage, break cadence, detour setting, and stop preferences locally so you can reuse them. You can edit these in the app. Removing the app removes its ordinary local app data.

Service providers and retention

Our service runs on Vercel. Hosting and mapping providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request metadata to operate, secure, and troubleshoot their services. The current application code does not create a trip-history database or user accounts. Provider logs and retention are governed by their services and settings.

We do not use advertising SDKs or sell personal information. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you include and retain correspondence as needed to handle your request.

Your choices and contact

You can use manual addresses, revoke location permission, clear local app data, and contact us about access or deletion of support correspondence at patrick@cairngtm.com. Avoid including sensitive information in feedback.

See the Google Maps Additional Terms of Service, Google's privacy policy and Vercel's privacy policy for their practices.
